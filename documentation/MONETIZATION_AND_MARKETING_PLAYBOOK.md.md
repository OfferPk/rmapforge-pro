# RMAPForge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

The SpaceWire and RMAP market is critical across government civil space agencies (NASA, ESA, JAXA) and commercial space launch / satellite primes (Airbus Defence & Space, Thales Alenia Space, Maxar, Northrop Grumman). Traditional SpaceWire and RMAP hardware test sets (STAR-Dundee, 4Links) cost \$25,000 to \$65,000 per bench setup. RMAPForge Pro provides an accessible, browser-native development and compliance verification environment for space instrument engineers, flight software developers, and payload integration teams.

## 2\. Commercial Pricing Tiers

- **Space Systems Engineer Seat**: \$149/month (Interactive studio, headless Python CLI, dual CRC-8 calculator).  
- **Spacecraft Integration Lab License**: \$1,490/month (\$12,500/year perpetual) including ECSS-E-ST-50-52C verification test suites, SpaceWire packet dissector templates, and dedicated aerospace engineering support.
